17. CINEMATIC HUMAN REALISM

The world must feel lived, not designed.

People are not models.
They are inhabitants of history.

Every face carries a story:
natural skin texture,
small imperfections,
different ages,
different emotions.

Scenes must feel captured by a real historical film crew.

Camera observes life unfolding.

No posed characters.
No theatrical acting.
No artificial beauty.

The image should feel like a frame from a historical feature film,
not an AI reconstruction.

Visual references:
The Passion of the Christ,
The Chosen,
Mary Magdalene,
historical documentary cinematography.

Prioritize:
human emotion,
natural behavior,
physical reality,
environmental storytelling.
