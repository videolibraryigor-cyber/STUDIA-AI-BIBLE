# MASTER_STYLE_ENHANCEMENT_v2.1
# Cinematic Quality Enhancement Layer
# Дополнительный слой качества. Не заменяет MASTER_STYLE_v2.0

## 1. OPTICAL CHARACTER (Оптический характер)

The image must retain the feeling of a real cinematic photograph captured by a professional historical film crew.

Use:

ARRI Alexa 65 cinematic image response,
Cooke Full Frame Anamorphic lens character,
natural anamorphic depth,
subtle lens breathing,
organic focus transition,
natural optical imperfections,
soft highlight halation,
gentle edge falloff,
filmic separation,
real lens behavior.

Avoid:

digital sharpness,
artificial clarity,
synthetic bokeh,
CGI lens effects,
perfectly clean digital image.

## 1A. OPTICAL RESOLUTION & MICRODETAIL LOCK
## Оптическое разрешение и микродетализация

The image must preserve extremely high optical detail while remaining physically photographic.

OPTICS:
ARRI Alexa 65 large-format cinematic image response,
Cooke Full Frame Anamorphic lens character,
razor-sharp optical focus on the intended focal plane,
crisp subject clarity,
natural anamorphic depth,
precise fine-detail rendering,
organic optical separation.

Sharpness must come from authentic optical focus and resolved physical texture,
never from digital sharpening or artificial clarity.

MATERIAL MICRODETAIL:
hand-chiseled porous limestone grain,
coarse woven linen fibers,
natural wool fibers,
aged leather grain,
individual skin pores,
fine beard texture,
subtle wrinkles,
parchment fibers,
wood grain,
wax imperfections,
dust particles,
small erosion marks,
real surface wear.

LIGHT:
raking light creating tactile relief,
physically motivated directional light,
natural microcontrast,
living fire illumination,
subtle highlight rolloff,
realistic shadow transitions.

The camera must resolve physical surface structure without making materials look artificially enhanced.

FORBIDDEN:
digital sharpening,
oversharpened edges,
artificial clarity,
texture hallucination,
CGI microdetail,
plastic surfaces,
synthetic skin,
excessive local contrast.


## 2. MATERIAL MICRODETAIL (Микродетали материалов)

Enhance realistic physical materials without exaggeration.

Show:

visible limestone pores,
hand-cut stone irregularities,
natural erosion marks,
aged wood grain,
cedar texture,
olive wood imperfections,
woven linen fibers,
wool structure,
natural leather wear,
parchment wrinkles,
aged paper texture,
wax imperfections,
dust accumulated in corners,
small traces of human interaction.

Materials must feel touched, used and historically authentic.

Avoid:

perfect surfaces,
plastic materials,
modern processing,
glossy artificial textures.


## 3. HISTORICAL REALISM ENHANCEMENT (Исторический реализм)

Every environment must feel physically inhabited and historically real.

Include:

natural imperfections,
uneven surfaces,
small architectural irregularities,
signs of human presence,
realistic scale,
archaeological authenticity,
period-accurate materials,
period-accurate construction methods.

The world must feel discovered, not designed.


## 4. ATMOSPHERIC DEPTH (Атмосфера)

Use subtle natural atmosphere:

thin airborne dust,
soft volumetric sunlight,
gentle atmospheric perspective,
natural distance haze,
warm air in daylight scenes,
cool moisture in night scenes,
quiet ancient atmosphere.

Atmosphere must remain physically believable.

Avoid:

heavy fog,
dramatic smoke,
fantasy atmosphere,
excessive rays,
artificial cinematic haze.


## 5. SKIN REALISM ENHANCEMENT (Реализм кожи)

Human faces must remain completely natural.

Show:

real skin pores,
natural wrinkles,
small facial asymmetry,
subtle age details,
natural eye moisture,
realistic beard texture,
authentic Mediterranean skin tones.

Avoid:

beauty retouch,
plastic skin,
wax appearance,
perfect symmetry,
AI face,
fashion photography skin.


## 6. LIGHT QUALITY ENHANCEMENT (Качество света)

Maintain physically motivated lighting.

Light sources:

sun,
moon,
window light,
beeswax candle,
oil lamp,
torch fire.

Enhance:

soft highlight rolloff,
natural shadow transitions,
realistic bounce light,
subtle warm and cool contrast,
physically accurate exposure.

Avoid:

invisible light sources,
studio lighting,
HDR lighting,
unrealistic glow,
oversaturated highlights.


## 7. COLOR SCIENCE (Цветовая наука)

Maintain cinematic natural color response.

Use:

ARRI-style color science,
natural highlight rolloff,
warm realistic highlights,
slightly cooler natural shadows,
balanced white balance,
restrained saturation,
organic film colors.

Preferred palette:

warm sandstone,
aged parchment,
cedar brown,
olive linen,
soft ivory,
weathered limestone,
dust gold.

Avoid:

cyan,
teal,
purple tint,
magenta cast,
neon colors,
oversaturated orange,
oversaturated blue.


## 8. FILM GRAIN AND IMAGE TEXTURE (Фактура изображения)

Use:

subtle cinematic film grain,
natural photographic texture,
organic image structure,
realistic exposure variation.

Avoid:

digital noise,
artificial grain overlay,
excessive texture.


## 9. HUMAN SCALE AND CAMERA OBSERVATION (Человеческий масштаб)

The camera observes the world, it does not show off.

Prioritize:

quiet observation,
human emotion,
natural movement,
authentic reactions,
small gestures,
meaningful pauses.

Avoid:

spectacle shots without story,
hero poses,
commercial advertising style.


## 10. FINAL DIRECTOR QUALITY CHECK (Проверка)

Before approving the frame:

Does it look photographed?
Does it feel historically possible?
Does the lighting exist inside the world?
Do materials feel physically real?
Do people feel like real humans?

If the image is beautiful but feels artificial, the frame is rejected.


## MASTER RULE

Enhance realism, never exaggerate realism.

The goal is not a perfect image.

The goal is a believable moment from a real historical world.
