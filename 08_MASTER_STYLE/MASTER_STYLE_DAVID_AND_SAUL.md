# MASTER VIDEO STYLE — DAVID & SAUL PROJECT v1.0 (MILITARY & ROYAL EPIC)

**Genre (Жанр):** Historical Biblical Military Epic / Royal Tragedy  
**Era (Эпоха):** 11th Century BCE (Iron Age I Levant: Israel & Philistia)  
**Tone & Scale (Масштаб):** Cinematic Grandeur, Dynamic Action, Cavalry Warfare, Authentic Biblical Narrative (1 Samuel 27–31)  
**Status:** `MANDATORY STUDIO STANDARD`

---

## 1. VISUAL SCALE & ACTION DIRECTIVES (Масштаб и действие)
* **No Quiet Wandering:** David is NOT a solitary peasant wanderer. He is a formidable military commander of 600 elite warriors («Храбрые Давида»). All major journeys are conducted **on horseback in combat armor**.
* **Massive Environments:** Fortress cities (Gath, Ziklag, Bethlehem) must feature monumental architecture (cyclopean stone, high towers, bronze gates). Armies must show hundreds of cavalrymen, war chariots, and endless tent camps.
* **Composition Ratio:**
  * **80%:** Dynamic Action, Cavalry Tracking, Master Establishing Shots, Diplomatic Interactions, Battles.
  * **20% Maximum:** Emotional Close-ups (strictly reserved for pivotal dramatic turning points, not routine filler).

---

## 2. CAMERA & OPTICS (Камера и оптика)
* **Body:** ARRI Alexa 65 large-format sensor.
* **Lenses:** Cooke Full Frame Anamorphic 2.39:1 (CinemaScope).
* **Focus & Sharpness:** Razor-sharp optical focus on subjects, organic natural bokeh on backgrounds, no digital blur.
* **Camera Movement:** Deliberate, epic single-vector cinematic movement (crane rises, lateral vehicle-speed tracking, slow steadi-push, majestic helicopter-style orbits).

---

## 3. CHARACTERS & WARDROBE LOCK (Фиксация персонажей)
* **David (CHAR_DAVID_WARRIOR):** 28yo, athletic, textured dark beard, resolute eyes. Wears hardened dark leather lamellar cuirass with copper rivets over dark linen tunic, leather bracers, iron broadsword, dark wool cloak. Rides a muscular dark Arabian warhorse.
* **King Saul (CHAR_SAUL_REGAL):** 58yo, imposing monarch. Wears heavy forged bronze scale armor, Tyrian purple wool mantle with embroidered gold borders and lion fibula, hammered gold/bronze diadem crown. Slain in fierce battle by Philistine archers on Mount Gilboa.
* **King Achish (CHAR_ACHISH):** 42yo, Aegean-Mycenaean warlord king. Wears cast bronze anatomical cuirass, feathered crested helmet, woven kilt with crimson trim.
* **Philistine Army:** Bronze scale armor, tall feathered crested helmets, two-wheeled war chariots, round embossed bronze shields.

---

## 4. LIGHTING & ENVIRONMENT (Свет и среда)
* **Lighting Physics:** Physically motivated natural light only (raking low-angle golden hour sunlight, stormy slate-grey battle skies, pitch torches with flying sparks, cave ember glow).
* **Atmosphere:** Natural airborne dust storms from horses' hooves, drifting morning ground mist, thick battle smoke, heat haze. No fake CGI glows or magical lasers.

---

## 5. VIDEO AI MOTION RULES (Правила генерации видео)
* **No Shouting / Screaming:** Never use "shouting" or "screaming" in video prompts (prevents open-mouth facial distortions). Use closed-mouth intense grimaces, fierce eye contact, firm physical gestures.
* **Single Camera Vector:** Strictly one clear camera motion per prompt.
* **Physical Cadence:** Natural horse gallop physics, billowing wool cloaks, spears held firmly, stable anatomy, 24fps filmic realism.
