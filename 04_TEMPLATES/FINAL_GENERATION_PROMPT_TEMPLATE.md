# FINAL GENERATION PROMPT TEMPLATE

**Purpose:** Clean, generator-ready prompt format (no metadata, no checklists, no technical documentation)

**Instructions:** This template shows the optimal structure for Image Prompt delivery to image generation systems. Replace example text with shot-specific content. Extract only from assembly process outputs.

---

## STORY

[Establish narrative context, dramatic purpose, emotional goal in 3-4 flowing sentences]

Example:
Establish Nicodemus in his private library at sunset, defining his scholarly routine and the calm order of his world before the first hint of curiosity and disruption. The dramatic beat is "Curiosity (Opening)" — the ordinary world before change. Convey contemplative calm and quiet confidence, with a subtle suggestion that the environment itself is about to become a space of observation rather than only comfort.

---

## CHARACTER

[Character identity, appearance, and performance state in natural prose]

Example:
Nicodemus, age 60-62, Jewish teacher of the Law and member of the Sanhedrin. Long intelligent face with high forehead, deep thoughtful brown eyes with slight crow's feet from decades of reading. Medium-length gray beard, well-kept and showing wisdom. Authentic Mediterranean skin tones with golden undertones, weathered from sun exposure. Wearing cream/off-white linen tunic, dark wool cloak, simple belt, leather sandals, and bronze signet ring showing his authority. Movement is measured and dignified, walking with confidence of someone comfortable in familiar space. Expression calm and composed, slightly expectant, with quiet awareness of familiar surroundings. Eyes scan the interior thoughtfully. No theatrical display; all emotion restrained and subtle.

## ENVIRONMENT

[Location, materials, and atmospheric quality in descriptive prose]

Example:
Private scroll library and study room in Jerusalem, first century CE. Warm limestone ashlar walls with hand-cut blocks showing natural archaeological age and irregularities. Cedar beam ceiling with authentic wood grain visible. Worn stone floor bearing marks of centuries of footsteps. High narrow window on wall allows sunset light to enter. Interior contains cedar wood desk positioned as central focal object, wooden shelves loaded with hundreds of parchment scrolls, clay jars for scroll storage, stone weights, oil lamps, and a lit beeswax candle on the desk. Dust particles visible in light shafts. Air feels ancient and still, with smell of old parchment, cedar wood, dust, and beeswax.

## CAMERA

[Cinematography and composition in direct descriptive language]

Example:
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with Cooke Full Frame Anamorphic lenses in CinemaScope 2.39:1 aspect ratio. Wide shot revealing full library interior, desk, scrolls, and Nicodemus in context. Camera positioned at eye level, approximately 160-170 cm from floor, providing observational and non-judgmental perspective. Distance approximately 4-5 meters showing Nicodemus and full room relationship. Shot size WIDE to establish location and character presence. Moderate depth of field maintaining legibility of both Nicodemus in foreground and desk, scrolls, candle in mid-ground. Movement is either static with locked-off framing, or slow gentle dolly in motivated by Nicodemus entering and camera acknowledging his arrival. If moving, approximately 0.5-1 meter movement over 5-second duration, straight line, smooth with no panning or rotation. Composition uses desk and shelves as lead lines drawing eye toward Nicodemus as he enters from right side. Negative space on left and background shows room and window, reinforcing quiet stability. Primary focus on Nicodemus as he moves; desk, scrolls, and candle remain legible in background. No rack focus required; steady observation throughout.

## LIGHTING

[Light sources, quality, direction, and physics in natural language]

Example:
Sunset light streaming through high window creates dominant side/back-lighting, warm golden tones approximately 3200-4000K. Beeswax candle on desk provides warm secondary fill light approximately 2000-2400K. No other light sources present; no studio lights, no hidden reflectors, no added illumination. Light direction from high window position creates side-lighting and edge-lighting on Nicodemus. Candle position near desk provides gentle warm fill on shadowed side of face. Effect creates three-dimensional modeling with warm shadows and golden highlights. Light quality shows soft-to-medium diffusion from window distance and soft glow from candle. Contrast ratio medium with warm highlights and cool shadows creating depth while maintaining readability. Natural soft rolloff of highlights from anamorphic optics, no harsh digital clipping. Shadow transitions natural with gradients, bounced light from limestone walls and light-colored tunic creating subtle fills. Warm bounce light from tunic and parchment onto shadowed side of face creates dimensional modeling. All lighting is physically motivated; window light from real sunset, candle light from object existing in scene. No studio setup, no artificial fill lights.

## NEGATIVE PROMPT

[Prohibited elements in direct list format, organized by category]

Example:
**Base Prohibitions:**
No modern objects (phones, electricity, glass, plastic, synthetic materials, contemporary furniture, automobiles, neon lights). No fantasy elements (magic, supernatural, mythological, fantasy creatures, fantasy architecture, CGI effects, impossible physics). No medieval style (suits of armor, medieval shields, crossed swords, castle stonework inconsistent with Roman Jerusalem, feudal settings). No artificial rendering (plastic appearance, wax skin, CGI face, AI artifacts, digital looking). No synthetic fabrics (polyester, modern weaves, synthetic dyes, neon-colored cloth). No glowing unnatural effects (glowing skin, unnatural bloom, excessive halation, fantasy glow, unrealistic highlights). No AI artifacts (extra fingers, duplicate people, impossible proportions, unnatural poses, morphing errors, texture artifacts). No continuity breaks (changing wardrobe, changing facial identity, changing age, changing beard, skin tone shift, hairstyle change). No technical breaks (exposure flicker, lighting shift, color shift, texture drift, lens change, aspect ratio change, camera jitter, white balance shift).

**Scene-Specific Prohibitions:**
No electric lighting fixtures. No modern scroll storage systems. No glass windows (only traditional wooden shuttered opening). No painted walls (only natural limestone aged surface). No varnished or stained wood (only natural aged cedar and olive). No theatrical staging (this is a moment of routine, not performance). No posed character (natural movement, not choreographed). No studio lighting (only window and candle, no reflectors, no fill lights). No fantasy Jerusalem (only archaeologically accurate first century). No Hollywood biblical style (no melodramatic performance, no orchestral moment, no artificial emotion).
