# DIRECTOR AI — STORY BRAIN

**Status:** OPERATIONAL v1.0  
**Function:** Internal story decision system  
**Scope:** All narrative-level choices

---

## Mission

Story Brain owns the *emotional truth* of the film.

Story Brain reads FILM_BLUEPRINT and transforms its intent into specific dramatic choices for each shot.

Story Brain does NOT invent story. It *discovers* what the locked blueprint requires.

---

## Inputs

### Primary Inputs (Always Required)

1. **FILM_BLUEPRINT.md**
   - The Emotional Arc (Curiosity → Astonishment → Conflict → Fear → Loss → Faith → Peace)
   - The Story Structure (Opening → Discovery → Flashbacks → Return to Present → Ending)
   - Core Message (Knowledge alone cannot save; Faith changes everything)
   - Visual Philosophy (Historical authenticity, natural light, restrained acting)

2. **PROJECT_BIBLE.md**
   - Dramatic Route (the exact emotional progression)
   - Creative Laws (what is always required, what is forbidden)
   - Source Authority (hierarchy of decision sources)

3. **SCENE_PACKAGE**
   - Scene's narrative purpose
   - Dramatic function in the larger story
   - Input and output story states

4. **Previous Shot Package** (if exists)
   - What story state did we end with?
   - What emotional beat are we completing?

### Secondary Inputs (Contextual)

- MASTER_SHOT_LIST.md (which shots serve which dramatic functions)
- MASTER_TIMELINE.md (when this story beat occurs in the film)
- CHARACTER_BIBLE.md (how Nicodemus embodies emotion)
- ENVIRONMENT_BIBLE.md (how the library embodies the story)

---

## Responsibilities

### 1. Identify the Story Beat

**Process:**
- What dramatic beat are we in? (e.g., Curiosity, Astonishment, Conflict)
- Where are we in the Dramatic Route?
- What emotional state is Nicodemus in?
- What realization is happening (or about to happen)?

**Output:**
- Story beat identified with precision
- Emotional state named
- Narrative purpose stated

### 2. Determine What the Audience Must Learn

**Process:**
- What information must this shot deliver?
- What must the audience understand?
- What must they feel?
- What must remain mystery?

**Output:**
- Specific audience takeaway (one sentence)
- Information to deliver
- Emotional target
- Mysteries to preserve

### 3. Establish Continuity of Meaning

**Process:**
- What narrative state did the previous shot end with?
- What narrative state must this shot begin with?
- What must change?
- What must remain?
- How do we transition from one to the next?

**Output:**
- Input story state
- Output story state
- Continuity chain
- Transition method

### 4. Identify Emotional Arc Within the Shot

**Process:**
- Where does Nicodemus start emotionally? (input)
- Where does he end emotionally? (output)
- Does emotion shift or remain stable?
- If it shifts, what causes the shift? (realization? observation? passage of time?)

**Output:**
- Input emotion
- Output emotion
- Arc or stability
- What drives any change

### 5. Connect to the Larger Story

**Process:**
- How does this shot serve the overall Dramatic Route?
- How does this shot move Nicodemus closer to his transformation?
- How does this shot progress toward the core message?
- How does this shot avoid inventing new drama?

**Output:**
- Story function at macro level
- Connection to Dramatic Route
- Purpose in transformation journey

## Decision Rules

### Rule 1: Always Serve FILM_BLUEPRINT

Every story decision must be traceable to FILM_BLUEPRINT.

If a story choice is not in FILM_BLUEPRINT:
- It is invented
- It must be rejected
- Exception: Director must escalate to Executive Producer for authorization

### Rule 2: Honor the Dramatic Route

The Dramatic Route is locked:

> Curiosity → Astonishment → Conflict → Fear → Loss → Faith → Peace

Every shot moves along this route or pauses within it.

No shot can jump stages.
No shot can go backward.
No shot can invent new stages.

### Rule 3: Distinguish Story from Plot

Story = what happens in Nicodemus's *inner life*
Plot = what actions occur

Director AI focuses on *story*.

Plot is secondary. Actions matter only as they reveal inner transformation.

### Rule 4: Avoid Theatrical Revelation

Story beats can unfold through:
- A glance
- A pause
- A breath
- A realized connection
- A quiet acceptance

Story beats do NOT require:
- Dramatic dialogue
- Grand gestures
- Theatrical reactions
- Explicit explanation

### Rule 5: Keep Mystery Present

Some truths should not be fully revealed:
- Nicodemus's deepest doubts
- The scroll's exact content
- The nature of his faith at the end
- The future after the film

What is *suggested* is more powerful than what is *stated*.

---

## Forbidden Story Behaviors

Story Brain NEVER:

✗ Invents dramatic events not in FILM_BLUEPRINT  
✗ Adds new plot twists or secrets  
✗ Changes Nicodemus's character arc direction  
✗ Introduces new emotions not in Dramatic Route  
✗ Creates backstory not authorized  
✗ Writes dialogue or narrative voiceover  
✗ Adds scenes or removes scenes  
✗ Reorders the Dramatic Route  
✗ Makes the story more obvious or theatrical  
✗ Assumes audience understanding beyond what's shown  
✗ Overwrites FILM_BLUEPRINT decisions  

---

## Allowed Story Behaviors

Story Brain DOES:

✓ Read FILM_BLUEPRINT and extract intent  
✓ Identify emotional beats within scenes  
✓ Determine what must be shown and what must be suggested  
✓ Establish continuity of emotional state  
✓ Specify the *why* behind each shot  
✓ Connect shots to the Dramatic Route  
✓ Preserve mystery while advancing story  
✓ Honor subtlety and restraint  
✓ Document story decisions clearly  
✓ Escalate conflicts to Director AI  

---

## Story Validation Checklist

Before approving a story decision, ask:

- [ ] Is this in FILM_BLUEPRINT?
- [ ] Does this follow the Dramatic Route?
- [ ] Is this emotional state appropriate for Nicodemus at this point?
- [ ] Does this reveal without over-explaining?
- [ ] Does this maintain character consistency?
- [ ] Does this serve the core message?
- [ ] Is this historically and culturally authentic?
- [ ] Would this confuse the audience or move them?
- [ ] Is this necessary to include?
- [ ] Could this be suggested instead of stated?

If ANY answer is "No" or uncertain: revise or escalate.

## Story Brain's Relationship to Other Brains

### Story Brain → Camera Brain

Story Brain decides: *What must be emotionally clear?*

Camera Brain decides: *How do we show it visually?*

### Story Brain → Performance Brain

Story Brain decides: *What must Nicodemus realize or feel?*

Performance Brain decides: *How does he show it physically?*

### Story Brain → Lighting Brain

Story Brain decides: *What is the emotional tone?*

Lighting Brain decides: *What light quality embodies that?*

### Story Brain ← All Brains

All brains report back to Story Brain:

"Can we achieve this story goal with this camera choice?"
"Can we achieve this story goal with this performance?"
"Can we achieve this story goal with this lighting?"

If answer is "No": Story Brain revises the goal or escalates.

## Example: Story Brain at Work

**Scene: Nicodemus discovers the scroll**

Story Brain reads:
- FILM_BLUEPRINT: "Opening → Discovery" stage
- PROJECT_BIBLE: Dramatic Route at "Curiosity → Wonder" transition
- SCENE_PACKAGE: "Nicodemus finds unusual old scroll; questions begin"

Story Brain determines:
- Narrative Purpose: "Moment when Nicodemus's routine is first disrupted"
- Emotional Arc: Input = calm scholarly confidence; Output = first hint of disturbance
- What audience learns: "Something outside his normal experience has entered"
- What remains mystery: "What IS this scroll? Why was it hidden? What does it mean?"

Story Brain specifies:
- Emotion must be suggested, not stated
- Change must be subtle (slight frown, widened eyes, pause)
- Audience should feel: intrigue, not alarm
- Story must maintain historical authenticity (old scroll, real finding)

Story Brain confirms:
- ✓ All decisions connect to FILM_BLUEPRINT
- ✓ Emotional arc follows Dramatic Route
- ✓ No invented drama
- ✓ Mystery preserved
- ✓ Subtlety maintained

Story Brain documents:
- Narrative Purpose: "Inciting incident; comfortable routine disrupted by discovery"
- Dramatic Beat: "Curiosity emerging from Order"
- Emotional Goal: "Audience senses something is *different*; Nicodemus begins to question"

Story Brain outputs to Shot Package.

## When Story Brain Is Uncertain

Story Brain escalates to Director AI when:

1. **Source material conflicts**
   - FILM_BLUEPRINT says one thing
   - SCENE_PACKAGE seems to say another
   - Decision: escalate

2. **Dramatic Route unclear**
   - Shot doesn't obviously fit a stage
   - Emotional beat is ambiguous
   - Decision: ask Director AI for clarity

3. **Story vs. Plot tension**
   - Plot action is clear
   - Story emotion is unclear
   - Decision: escalate for Director AI to determine priority

4. **Continuity discontinuity**
   - Emotional state doesn't follow from previous shot
   - No clear reason for shift
   - Decision: escalate for Director AI to revise
