**Forbidden:** Creating scenes, events, or dramatic beats not in FILM_BLUEPRINT

- "Insert a scene showing Nicodemus's fear externalized"

### CATEGORY 3: Disrespecting History

#### 3.1 Never introduce anachronisms

**Forbidden:** Allowing modern objects, clothing, technology, or architecture visible in shots

**Examples of forbidden:**
- Glass windows (rare in 1st century CE)
- Modern furniture design language
- Modern materials (plastic, vinyl, concrete)
- Medieval architecture (should be 1st century Roman/Jewish)
- Modern religious iconography (halos, glowing crosses)

**Acceptable:** Researching 1st century CE materials and design

---

#### 3.2 Never ignore historical accuracy

**Forbidden:** Violating historical authenticity for visual appeal

**Examples of forbidden:**
- "It looks more cinematic with that inaccurate material"
- "The modern design fits the mood better"
- "We can excuse the anachronism for dramatic effect"

**Acceptable:** Refusing shots that violate historical accuracy, even if beautiful

---

#### 3.3 Never use Hollywood "biblical" aesthetic

**Forbidden:** Employing cinematic Bible film conventions

**Examples of forbidden:**
- Magical glowing lights
- Halos or divine visual effects
- Gold ornament excess
- Victorian or medieval design (not 1st century)
- Theatrical spectacle over historical accuracy

**Acceptable:** Historical authenticity and restrained visual language

---

### CATEGORY 5: Abandoning Restraint

#### 5.1 Never create beautiful but meaningless shots

**Forbidden:** Pursuing visual spectacle detached from story purpose

**Examples of forbidden:**
- "This shot is stunning visually even if the story doesn't need it"
- "Add this camera move because it's cinematic"
- "Include this effect because it looks impressive"

**Rule:** If story doesn't need it, it's forbidden.

---

#### 5.2 Never use camera tricks without motivation

**Forbidden:** Employing camera techniques for their own sake

**Examples of forbidden:**
- Dutch angles (tilted horizon) without emotional motivation
- Handheld shakiness without instability in story
- Extreme depth of field for aesthetic effect
- Lens flare or bloom without dramatic purpose

**Rule:** Every technical choice must be story-motivated.

---

#### 5.3 Never add theatrical effects

**Forbidden:** Introducing visual or performance tricks that undermine authenticity

**Examples of forbidden:**
- Dramatic orchestral music video energy
- Over-the-top emotional reactions
- Special effects without historical basis
- Unmotivated studio lighting

**Rule:** Authenticity and restraint always.

## The Test

Before creating any output or making any decision, ask:

**"Is this action on the Forbidden list?"**

- Yes → STOP. Do not proceed. Escalate.
- No → Check Authority document. Proceed only if within authority.

**"Can I justify this action using FILM_BLUEPRINT or locked Bibles?"**

- Yes → Proceed with documentation
- No → STOP. This is forbidden.

**"Does this violate any governance rule?"**

- Yes → STOP. Escalate to Executive Producer.
- No → Proceed with caution.
