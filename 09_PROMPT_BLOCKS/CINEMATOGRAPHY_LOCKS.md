# CINEMATOGRAPHY LOCKS
## AI FILM STUDIO

---

# MASTER RULE

Every shot must look as if photographed by the same cinematographer.

Story before beauty.

Emotion before spectacle.

Movement only when motivated.

Every frame has one visual subject.

---

# CAMERA STYLE

Slow.

Patient.

Observational.

Respectful.

Never flashy.

Never music-video style.

---

# CAMERA MOVEMENT

Allowed

Locked-off

Slow Push-in

Slow Pull-back

Slow Dolly

Slow Tracking

Slow Crane

Slow Overhead

Slow Pan

Slow Tilt

---

Never

Fast Dolly

Fast Pan

Fast Tilt

Whip Pan

Fast Crane

Drone Racing

Handheld Shake

Music Video Camera

Random Movement

---

# SHOT COMPOSITION

Simple.

Balanced.

Natural.

Negative space.

Leading lines.

Foreground depth.

Background depth.

Human scale.

---

# LENSES

Cooke Full Frame Anamorphic.

Natural distortion.

Organic falloff.

Soft cinematic rendering.

Never ultra sharp digital.

---

# FOCUS

Natural depth of field.

Slow rack focus only.

No aggressive focus pulls.

Eyes remain priority.

---

# LIGHT

Light always motivates the frame.

No decorative lighting.

No random highlights.

Natural practical sources only.

---

# COLOR

Muted.

Earth tones.

Historical realism.

Natural skin.

Stone.

Wood.

Linen.

Olive trees.

Dust.

No fantasy palette.

---

# EDITING RHYTHM

Slow.

Breathing.

Every cut has purpose.

Hold shots longer.

Allow silence.

Do not rush.

---

# VISUAL LANGUAGE

The camera observes.

It never comments.

It never judges.

It simply witnesses history.

---

# FINAL RULE

If a shot attracts attention to the camera instead of the story,

the shot is wrong.
