GLOBAL RULES
(Глобальные правила)
ЭТОТ БЛОК ВСТАВЛЯЕТСЯ В КАЖДЫЙ IMAGE PROMPT.
CONTINUITY LOCK

Maintain identical lighting continuity across the entire scene.

No lighting drift.

No exposure drift.

No white balance drift.

No color grading drift.

No palette drift.

Same atmosphere.

Same weather.

Same time of day.

Same motivated light sources.

Same shadow direction.

Same shadow density.

Same contrast ratio.

Same highlight rolloff.

Same camera response.

Same lens rendering.

Every shot must appear photographed by the same cinematographer, on the same camera, during the same continuous take.

CAMERA LOCK
(Фиксация камеры)
Во всех сценах

ARRI Alexa 65

Cooke Full Frame Anamorphic

CinemaScope 2.39:1

Natural film grain

Realistic highlight rolloff

No HDR look

Photochemical cinematic color science

COLOR LOCK
(Фиксация цвета)
Во всех сценах

Natural skin tones

Authentic limestone colors

No fantasy colors

No teal and orange look

No oversaturation

No artificial contrast

Natural cinematic palette

ATMOSPHERE LOCK
(Фиксация атмосферы)
Во всех сценах

No random fog

No random dust

No random sunlight

No random glow

No fantasy atmosphere

Only physically accurate light behavior

Only historically accurate atmosphere

MASTER RULE
If this scene contains multiple shots,
lighting MUST remain visually identical
between every shot.

Only camera position and subject movement may change.

Lighting never changes unless the screenplay explicitly changes time or location.
