JESUS MASTER REFERENCE v1.0.md

(Мастер-референс Иисуса)

1. IDENTITY (Идентичность)
Character:
Jesus of Nazareth
Historical period:
1st century Judea, Second Temple period
Age:
approximately 30-33 years old
Role:
Jewish teacher, rabbi, Messiah figure according to the Gospel narrative
Visual approach:
historically grounded human representation,
not iconographic,
not fantasy,
not idealized religious painting.

2. FACE DESIGN (Дизайн лица)
Face:
long oval face,
Mediterranean Jewish features,
calm intelligent eyes,
natural facial asymmetry.
Eyes:
deep,
compassionate,
observant,
quiet authority.
Expression:
restrained emotion,
peaceful presence,
rarely smiling,
communicates through eyes and silence.
Skin:
natural pores,
sun exposure,
small imperfections,
real human texture.
Forbidden:
perfect beauty,
plastic skin,
fashion model appearance.

3. HAIR & BEARD (Волосы и борода)
Hair:
dark brown almost black,
medium-long,
natural waves,
shoulder length.
Texture:
slightly dry from Mediterranean climate,
not styled.
Beard:
full but natural,
trimmed but not modern groomed.
4. BODY & MOVEMENT (Пластика)
Height:
approximately 175-180 cm
Build:
lean,
working man's body,
not muscular hero.
Movement:
slow,
calm,
purposeful.
No theatrical gestures.
5. CLOTHING (Одежда)
Inner tunic:
natural undyed linen,
soft ivory.
Outer mantle:
rough woven wool,
beige / sand color.
Materials:
handwoven,
visible fibers,
natural wrinkles,
wear marks.
No luxury.

6. CINEMATIC PERFORMANCE (Кино-пластика)
Jesus should feel like:
a real person living in first century Judea.
Camera observes him.
He does not perform for camera.
Important:
silence,
breathing,
eyes,
small movements of hands.

7. LIGHTING REFERENCE
Day:
warm Mediterranean sunlight,
reflected Jerusalem limestone light.
Night:
torchlight,
oil lamps,
moonlight contrast.
Always:
light exists inside the world.

8. NEGATIVE CHARACTER RULES
Do not create:
modern Jesus,
European Renaissance Jesus,
perfect angelic face,
glowing aura,
fantasy Messiah,
Hollywood superhero,
plastic skin,
perfect symmetry.
