## Director's intention

The audience must experience the discovery through Nicodemus' eyes. The reveal comes in his focused reading, the slow motion of his hand on the scroll, and the widening of his gaze. Astonishment is internal, visible in small changes to expression and breath.

## Scene continuity

Input State: Nicodemus is seated at the cedar desk, scroll unrolled before him, and the library feels quiet but charged.
Dramatic Action: He reads the scroll carefully, realizes the text is unexpected and significant, and reacts with restrained shock.
Output State: Nicodemus is no longer merely curious. He is astonished, engaged, and on the verge of deeper questioning.
