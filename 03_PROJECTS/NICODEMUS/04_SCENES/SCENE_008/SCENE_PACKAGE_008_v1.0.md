## Director's intention

This scene should feel calm and resolute. Nicodemus is still in the library, but he is no longer restless. The scroll is treated with reverence, and the action of returning it to the shelf must feel like a quiet ritual.

## Scene continuity

Input State: Nicodemus has recognized the truth in his memory and is ready to accept its meaning.
Dramatic Action: He settles at his desk, touches the scroll gently, and carefully stores it back among the shelves.
Output State: The scene ends with Nicodemus carefully closing the scroll and returning it to the shelf. He stands in the transformed library at peace, prepared to live with what he now believes.
