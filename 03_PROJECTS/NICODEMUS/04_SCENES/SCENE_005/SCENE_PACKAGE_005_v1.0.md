## Director's intention

Nicodemus is still in the library, but his gaze shifts outward. This scene should feel like the first step toward leaving the cocoon of his study. The window becomes a metaphor for the world beyond the scroll.

## Scene continuity

Input State: Nicodemus is still holding the scroll and is now aware his private study is not enough.
Dramatic Action: He moves to the window, looks out at the city, and reflects on the implications of what he has learned.
Output State: The scene ends with Nicodemus balancing the scroll against the outside world. He is now seeking, not merely reacting.
