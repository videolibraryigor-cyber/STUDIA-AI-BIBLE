## Director's intention

Nicodemus is shown in his familiar world: the library is his sanctuary and work is his comfort. This scene must feel calm and grounded, with no theatricality. The audience begins to sense that he is a man of order and routine, and that something small in his environment will soon disturb this quiet confidence.

## Scene continuity

Input State: Nicodemus is alone in the library at sunset. He is composed, focused, and comfortable in his routine. The library is orderly, lit by warm window light and a single candle.
Dramatic Action: Nicodemus enters, surveys the room, and moves toward his desk. His hands touch scrolls and objects with familiarity.
Output State: The scene ends with Nicodemus settled at his desk and the viewer aware that his scholarly world is established. His curiosity is only hinted at, but the library is now a place of observation rather than mere comfort.
