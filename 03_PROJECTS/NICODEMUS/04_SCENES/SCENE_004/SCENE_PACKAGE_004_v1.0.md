## Director's intention

This scene should feel restless within the same space. Nicodemus cannot stay still, and his movement reflects his unsettled mind. The library remains the setting, but the emotional tone shifts toward doubt and a sense that answers are now required.

## Scene continuity

Input State: Nicodemus has read the scroll and is holding it in his hands. He is still in the library, but his calm is fractured.
Dramatic Action: He stands and moves through the room, re-rolling the scroll and reconsidering what it means.
Output State: The scene ends with Nicodemus preserving the scroll and his doubt now established. The library feels smaller, as if the walls are closing in on him.
