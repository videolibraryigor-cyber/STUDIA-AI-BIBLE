## Director's intention

This is the emotional low point. The library feels like a trap, and Nicodemus is overwhelmed by the implications of what he has discovered. Fear should be shown through stillness, breath, and the way his hands grip the desk.

## Scene continuity

Input State: Nicodemus has taken the scroll back to his desk and is alone with the growing weight of its meaning.
Dramatic Action: He struggles internally, his body reacting to fear as he imagines the consequences of believing the scroll.
Output State: The scene ends with the library feeling darker and Nicodemus still grappling with the possibility that his world has changed.
