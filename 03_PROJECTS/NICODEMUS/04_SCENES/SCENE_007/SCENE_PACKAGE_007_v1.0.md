## Director's intention

This scene should feel like an internal flashback. The memory is not a fantasy spectacle but a quiet, honest recognition that the scroll matches something Nicodemus has already lived. It is the moment the story moves from fear into understanding.

## Scene continuity

Input State: Nicodemus is still in the library, weighed down by the scroll and his doubts.
Dramatic Action: A memory surfaces and he connects it to the scroll's testimony; the scroll's illustrated story becomes a living memory and his expression changes as he realizes the truth has been inside him already.
Output State: The scene ends with Nicodemus returning to the desk with a new inner certainty. The crisis has been softened by understanding and the path to faith is opening.
