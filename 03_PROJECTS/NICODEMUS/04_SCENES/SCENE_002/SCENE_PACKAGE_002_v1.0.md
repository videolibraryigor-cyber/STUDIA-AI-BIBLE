## Director's intention

The scroll appears as a quiet discovery. The scene should feel intimate and restrained: the magic is not in spectacle but in the attention paid to a simple object. Nicodemus' eyes and hands carry the emotion. The audience should feel the first tremor of unease beneath his scholarly curiosity.

## Scene continuity

Input State: The library is now night, candlelit, and Nicodemus is searching among familiar shelves.
Dramatic Action: He discovers an unfamiliar scroll hidden behind others, draws it out carefully, and examines it with growing interest.
Output State: The scroll is in his hands and his expression shifts from routine to curiosity. The object is now a dramatic anchor for the story.
