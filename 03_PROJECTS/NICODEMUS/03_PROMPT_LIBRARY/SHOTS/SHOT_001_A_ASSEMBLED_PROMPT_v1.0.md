## 1. STORY & CREATIVE INTENT
### Narrative Purpose

Establish Nicodemus in his private library at sunset, defining his scholarly routine and the calm order of his world before the first hint of curiosity and disruption.

### Dramatic Beat

Curiosity (Opening) — the ordinary world before change, as defined by `FILM_BLUEPRINT.md` and `PROJECT_BIBLE.md`.

### Emotional Goal

Convey contemplative calm and quiet confidence, with a subtle suggestion that the environment itself is about to become a space of observation rather than only comfort.

### Director's Intention

Show Nicodemus in his familiar sanctuary with restrained dignity. The library should feel stable and grounded; the shot must make the audience aware of his ordered routine while leaving room for a later shift toward curiosity.

## 2. SPATIAL STAGING & COMPOSITION (3-Layer Depth)
### Shot Size

WIDE — to reveal the library interior, the desk, and Nicodemus entering the space.

### Lens

Normal-to-slight-wide focal intention with moderate depth of field to keep both Nicodemus and room details readable.

### Camera Height

Eye level — observational and non-judgmental.

### Distance

Observational — enough distance to show environment and character relationships without being intrusive.

### Movement

Static or slow dolly in, motivated by the character entering and the camera gently acknowledging the established study.

### Composition

Leading lines formed by the desk and shelves draw the eye toward Nicodemus as he enters from the right. Negative space to the left allows the room and window to breathe, reinforcing the quiet stability of the library.

### Framing

Include the desk, high window, shelves, and the entrance path. Full room coverage with Nicodemus entering into the central quarter of the frame.

### Focus Strategy

Primary focus on Nicodemus as he enters; maintain enough depth to keep the desk, scrolls, and candle legible. No rack focus is required in this establishing shot.

### Blocking

- Nicodemus enters from the right side of the room.
- He moves toward the desk, appearing deliberate and measured.
- Hands remain relaxed at his sides or slightly forward as he enters.
- The candle and desk remain stationary; the window is visible behind or beside him depending on angle.

## 4. LIGHTING & SCENOGRAPHY
### Time of Day

Sunset / late evening.

### Light Sources

- Window sunlight at sunset.
- Desk candle.

### Direction

- Dominant light from the window: side/back light from high wall.
- Fill from the candle: warm, near the desk.

### Contrast

Soft-to-medium contrast: enough shadow to feel volumetric, but not harsh; the room remains warm and readable.

### Color Temperature

Warm amber from candlelight; warm golden edge from sunset through the window; balanced to preserve historical authenticity.

# MASTER MOTION RULE (Главное правило движения)

Only ONE primary motion vector per 3–5 second generation clip.

Never mix complex camera movement with large character body movement and heavy environmental physics in a single AI generation.

---

# MOTION VECTORS (Векторы движения)

### 1. CAMERA VECTOR (Вектор камеры)
* Allowed: Imperceptible slow push-in, gentle dolly in (max 0.5m), subtle lateral drift.
* Character state: Static or subtle breathing only.
* Environment: Subtle flame/dust motion.

### 2. CHARACTER VECTOR (Вектор персонажа)
* Allowed: Eyeline shift, subtle head turn, slow hand hesitation, finger adjustment on prop, visible slow inhale/exhale.
* Camera state: Locked-off / completely static tripod.

### 3. ENVIRONMENT VECTOR (Вектор среды)
* Allowed: Candle flame flicker, drifting dust motes in sunbeam, subtle fabric drape vibration.
* Camera & Character: Static.

---

# KEYFRAME CONTROL (First Frame → Last Frame)

For character and action transitions, use dual keyframe interpolation:
* `Start Keyframe`: Exact established state.
* `End Keyframe`: Target state.
* Video engine generates only intermediate physical movement.

---

# PROHIBITED IN VIDEO GENERATION (Строго запрещено)

* ❌ Face warping / facial structure mutation (сохранять 100% геометрию лица).
* ❌ Character morphing / costume changes during clip.
* ❌ Fast or sudden camera panning / whip pans.
* ❌ Unnatural sliding / floaty feet motion.
* ❌ Digital glitching, limb multiplication, physics breaks.
* ❌ Sudden lighting, exposure, or color temperature shifts.
