## 1. Core Philosophy: Acoustic Realism & Emotional Restraint

Sound in *NICODEMUS* is not decoration. Sound is historical immersion and spiritual intimacy.

### The 3 Laws of Biblical Acoustic Reality:
1. **Silence is an Active Character:** The world before modern machinery was fundamentally quiet. Silence allows the audience to hear breathing, thought, and sacred stillness.
2. **Organic Physics Only:** Every sound must have a visible or historically motivated world source. No artificial synth risers, modern sub-bass drops, or metallic cinematic impacts.
3. **Tactile Micro-Details (Foley):** Natural ancient materials have distinct acoustic signatures. The dryness of papyrus, the friction of coarse linen, and the dense resonance of stone define the tactile reality.

## 2. Acoustic Environments (Пространственная акустика)

### `LOC_NICODEMUS_LIBRARY` (Частная библиотека Никодима)
* **Geometry & Materials:** Hand-cut limestone walls (ashlar blocks), heavy cedar wood ceiling beams, stone tile floor with thin dust layer.
* **Reverberation (RT60):** 0.8–1.1 seconds. Dense, warm, natural room decay without metallic flutter. High frequencies are absorbed by wooden shelves and stacked parchment scrolls.
* **Background Ambience:**
  * Distant Jerusalem night/sunset: very faint evening wind against narrow stone window slit, distant night insects (Jerusalem hills), no modern noise floor.
  * Internal room presence: subtle draft through window aperture, gentle flame hiss of beeswax candle.

## 3. Foley & Tactile Sound Palette (Библиотека шумов)

| Category | Material / Action | Acoustic Character | Emotional Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parchment / Scrolls** | Unrolling dry aged papyrus / parchment | Crisp, fibrous, dry friction, subtle paper crackle | Evokes ancient knowledge, fragility of truth |
| **Stone & Weights** | Limestone weight sliding on cedar desk | Heavy, low-frequency friction, dull stone resonance | Groundedness, permanence, routine |
| **Flame & Light** | Beeswax candle / olive oil lamp wick | Soft gentle flutter, micro-crackle of melting wax | Fragile life, passage of time, intimacy |
| **Footsteps** | Leather sandals on uneven stone slab | Soft leather scuff, dry grit/sand particle displacement | Measured pace, scholar's hesitation |
| **Textiles** | Coarse woven linen tunic & dark wool cloak | Heavy, dry fabric swish, non-synthetic rustle | Human presence, restrained movement |
| **Human Breath** | Slow measured nasal inhale / held breath | Intimate close-mic'd breath, micro-sighs | Vulnerability, realization, internal conflict |

## 4. Interaction with Musical Score (Взаимодействие с музыкой)

* Music drives editing and emotional flow (`MUSIC_SHOT_MAPPING.md`).
* Sound design ducks naturally during soaring vocal melodies.
* In contemplative pauses, foley elements (breath, parchment, candle) step forward to maintain narrative tension.
* **Mastering Target:** True Peak: -1.0 dBFS, Integrated LUFS: -16 LUFS (cinema / streaming standard).
