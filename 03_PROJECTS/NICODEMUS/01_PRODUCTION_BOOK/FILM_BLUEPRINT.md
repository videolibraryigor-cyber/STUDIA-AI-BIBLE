# 2. FILM PURPOSE (Цель фильма)

The film is not about historical reconstruction.

The film is about the inner transformation of Nicodemus.

Everything the audience sees exists only to reveal that transformation.

History serves emotion.

Emotion serves truth.

Truth serves the Gospel.

---

# 3. CORE MESSAGE (Главная мысль)

A man who knew the Scriptures
had to meet the Living Word.

Knowledge alone cannot save.

Faith changes everything.

# 7. VISUAL PHILOSOPHY (Визуальная философия)

Historical authenticity.

Natural light.

Restrained acting.

No spectacle.

No fantasy.

No visual effects without dramatic purpose.

# 8. CAMERA PHILOSOPHY (Философия камеры)

The camera observes.

The camera never performs.

The audience should feel present,
not entertained by camera tricks.

Camera movement is motivated only.

Every movement answers:

"Why?"

# 11. MUSIC PHILOSOPHY (Музыкальная философия)

Music drives editing.

Editing never fights music.

Visual rhythm follows emotional rhythm,

---

# 12. EDITING PHILOSOPHY (Философия монтажа)

Invisible editing.

Natural pacing.

Longer shots during contemplation.

Shorter shots during revelation.

No unnecessary transitions.

---

# 13. SCENE HIERARCHY (Иерархия сцен)

Every scene must answer:

Why does this scene exist?

Every shot must answer:

Why does this shot exist?

If no answer exists,

remove it.

---

# 14. SHOT HIERARCHY (Иерархия кадров)

Each shot has:

One purpose.

One emotion.

One visual focus.

One dramatic function.

Never two.

# PROJECT NORTH STAR (Главный ориентир проекта)

When any creative decision becomes difficult,
always ask:

"Will this help the audience understand Nicodemus' journey toward Christ?"

If the answer is NO,

do not include it.
