# MUSIC SYNC ENGINE PROPOSAL v1.0

Status:
DRAFT

Purpose:
Create a music-aware editing and directing system inside AI FILM STUDIO.

The Director AI must understand not only story and visuals, but also musical timing and lyric synchronization.

---

# Problem Statement

Current workflow can create visually correct scenes, but shots may not match:

- musical beats;
- strong accents;
- lyric meaning;
- emotional timing of the song.

A cinematic shot must satisfy three conditions:

1. Music timing.
2. Lyric meaning.
3. Story continuity.

A shot that fails one of these conditions requires revision.

---

# Master Audio Rules

The system must support:

Original Producer Audio:
- WAV source file

Edited Master Version:
- final editing timeline version

The active timeline must always have one defined audio authority.

---

# Shot Duration Rules

Music video shots should follow musical structure.

Preferred shot durations:

- 2.2 seconds
- 4.4 seconds

Shot changes should preferably happen on strong musical accents.

The system must avoid random shot lengths unless there is a strong cinematic reason.

---

# Lyric Synchronization Engine

Each shot must contain:

## Timecode

Example:

01:12.0 - 01:16.4

## Lyric Reference

Exact line of song.

## Visual Meaning

What the viewer should understand at this moment.

## Story Event

What happens in the biblical narrative.

## Shot Purpose

Why this shot exists.

---

# Validation Rules

Before approving a shot:

Check:

## Music

PASS / FAIL

- Does the shot align with beat structure?
- Does the cut happen naturally?

## Lyrics

PASS / FAIL

- Does the image correspond to the words being sung?

## Story

PASS / FAIL

- Does the event happen at the correct point in the narrative?

## Emotion

PASS / FAIL

- Does the shot support the emotional development?

---

# Editing Philosophy

The strongest image is not always the correct image.

Priority:

1. Producer script.
2. Biblical accuracy.
3. Lyrics.
4. Music rhythm.
5. Cinematic beauty.

---

# Goal

Create a Director AI that can design music videos where every shot is:

- musically synchronized;
- lyrically meaningful;
- historically accurate;
- emotionally purposeful.
